Write a JCP-style subsection defining the controlled stacked-box dielectric model used in the final Numerical Results section.

Purpose:
This model is used for end-to-end convergence, dielectric-parameter dependence, and scaling tests of the complete screened Coulomb integral solver. Unlike a single cube or an isolated slab, the model contains two dielectric boxes with a shared overlapping face. It is designed to represent a thin dielectric layer placed on top of a bulk dielectric substrate, while remaining geometrically simple and reproducible.

Model geometry:
Use two axis-aligned dielectric boxes with identical lateral footprint. The lower box is a 10 x 10 x 10 cube,

    Omega_2 = [-5,5] x [-5,5] x [0,10],

and the upper box is a 10 x 10 x 1 thin layer placed directly on top of it,

    Omega_1 = [-5,5] x [-5,5] x [10,11].

The two boxes share the full planar interface

    Gamma_12 = [-5,5] x [-5,5] x {10}.

The exterior region is vacuum with permittivity

    epsilon_0 = 1.

Use distinct dielectric constants for the two boxes, for example

    epsilon_1 = 4,      epsilon_2 = 10,

where Omega_1 denotes the thin upper layer and Omega_2 denotes the lower bulk substrate.

This geometry contains multiple dielectric regions, a material-material interface, exterior vacuum interfaces, edges, and corners. It also includes a large-aspect-ratio layer, since Omega_1 has dimensions 10 x 10 x 1.

Source and target densities:
Use smooth normalized Gaussian volume densities,

    rho_src(x)
    =
    (2 pi s^2)^(-3/2)
    exp( - |x - x_s|^2 / (2 s^2) ),

    rho_tar(x)
    =
    (2 pi s^2)^(-3/2)
    exp( - |x - x_t|^2 / (2 s^2) ).

For the baseline controlled test, place the densities in the vacuum/active region above the thin layer, close to the top surface but far from the lateral edges:

    x_s = (-0.35, 0, 11 + d),
    x_t = ( 0.35, 0, 11 + d),

with

    d = 0.3,     s = 0.12 or 0.15.

This placement produces a nontrivial screening correction from both the thin layer and the underlying bulk substrate, while keeping the source and target smooth and separated from all dielectric interfaces. It also avoids making the local physical signal dominated by the lateral edges.

If the intended application requires the source density to lie inside the upper layer, an alternative is to place the centers at z = 10.5 and explicitly treat the source region permittivity as epsilon_1. In that case, state the convention used for the incident potential normalization.

Quantity of interest:
Compute the screened Coulomb integral

    V = int rho_tar(x) phi_src(x) dx,

where phi_src is the electrostatic potential generated by rho_src in the presence of the stacked dielectric boxes. Also compute the vacuum reference

    V_vac = int rho_tar(x) u_inc(x) dx,

and the screening correction

    Delta V = V - V_vac.

This controlled quantity is a simplified analogue of the screened four-index integral in a layered dielectric environment.

Use in the numerical results:
1. End-to-end convergence:
   Fix the stacked-box geometry, densities, and dielectric parameters, for example epsilon_1 = 4 and epsilon_2 = 10. Fix all algorithmic parameters except the Gauss-Legendre order p and the edge-refinement depth r. Plot the final integral error E_V and GMRES iteration count versus r for several values of p.

2. Dielectric-parameter dependence:
   Keep the same geometry and densities. Either fix epsilon_1 = 4 and vary the substrate permittivity

       epsilon_2 = 1, 2, 5, 10, 20, 50,

   or fix epsilon_2 = 10 and vary the thin-layer permittivity

       epsilon_1 = 1, 2, 4, 8, 16, 32.

   Report V, Delta V, GMRES iteration count, and optionally solve time or BIE residual. The expected physical trend is that the screening correction vanishes when the varied permittivity equals the exterior value and increases in magnitude as the contrast grows.

3. Scaling:
   Use the same stacked-box geometry while increasing the boundary unknown count N_Gamma by increasing edge-refinement depth, changing p, or uniformly scaling the lateral dimensions at fixed resolution per unit length. Report CPU time and memory as functions of N_Gamma.

Manuscript points to emphasize:
1. The model contains two dielectric boxes sharing a full face, which tests material-material interface coupling in addition to dielectric-vacuum interfaces.
2. The upper 10 x 10 x 1 box gives a large-aspect-ratio layer, while the lower 10 x 10 x 10 box acts as a bulk substrate.
3. The Gaussian source and target densities make the convergence tests reproducible and avoid ambiguity from realistic orbital-pair density discretization.
4. The densities are placed near the top surface to generate a measurable screening correction from the complete stacked structure, but away from lateral edges to avoid an edge-dominated physical signal.
5. The full GW calculation is deferred to the final application subsection, where realistic densities and physical observables are used.
